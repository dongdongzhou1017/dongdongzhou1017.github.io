## Research

My research lies at the intersection of computer science and biomedical engineering, focusing on biomedical signal processing, brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), and efficient & interpretable deep learning for medical health monitoring. Specifically, my current research topics include:

1. **Sleep EEG Analysis & Automatic Sleep Stage Classification**: Single-channel and multimodal sleep scoring, class imbalance learning, and developmental non-stationarity modeling for pediatric sleep.
2. **Brain-Computer Interfaces & Neural Decoding**: EEG/EMG signal processing, time-frequency representation learning, and affective/cognitive brain decoding.
3. **Model Compression & Edge Intelligence**: Ultra-lightweight neural networks, spiking neural networks (SNNs), and real-time inference deployment on mobile and wearable edge devices.
4. **Interpretable Artificial Intelligence**: Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) and physiology-informed representation alignment for trustworthy clinical decision support.
